# Automatic assessment criticality
New assessments use Auto instead of a manual slider. The worker inspects selected container or locally available image metadata, evaluates the four existing business-impact factors, and stores the evidence in target_json and finding_json. Complete evidence is averaged and rounded half-up to an integer 1–5 for the existing risk formula. Configured disposable lab targets without business metadata use a provisional 1/5 assumption. Other targets with missing evidence remain unassigned and contextual risk is not calculated. Imported remote evidence does not establish business criticality. Existing assessments retain legacy scores. The existing remote evidence import is unchanged in scope; no remote scanning was added. Assessment-level scoring is not a full per-asset business inventory.
